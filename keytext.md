## Description
Tired of clicking "Accept" just to make cookie popups go away? CookieZap does it for you automatically.

CookieZap is a Safari extension that detects cookie consent popups and clicks "Reject" or "Necessary Only" buttons on your behalf. Unlike other tools that just hide banners with CSS, CookieZap actually rejects cookies, giving you real privacy protection.

SMART AUTO-REJECT
CookieZap intelligently finds and clicks "Reject All", "Decline", or "Necessary Only" buttons on cookie consent popups. It works with all major consent management platforms including OneTrust, Cookiebot, Quantcast, and dozens more.

THREE BLOCKING MODES
- Smart Reject: Finds and clicks reject buttons automatically
- Hide Only: Hides popups with CSS without interacting
- Block All: Blocks cookie-related network requests entirely

REAL-TIME DASHBOARD
See exactly how many popups you have zapped. Track today's count, total blocked, and success rate. Watch your numbers grow as you browse.

WHITELIST CONTROL
Need to see cookie popups on a specific site? Add it to your whitelist and CookieZap will skip it.

SAFARI TOOLBAR POPUP
Quickly check protection status, pause blocking, or whitelist the current site right from Safari's toolbar.

ZERO CONFIGURATION
Install the extension, enable it in Safari settings, and you are done. No accounts, no sign-ups, no complicated setup. CookieZap works immediately.

PRIVACY FIRST
CookieZap runs entirely on your device. No data is collected, no servers are contacted, no tracking. Your browsing stays private.

One-time purchase. No subscription. No ads. No data collection. Just clean, uninterrupted browsing.
