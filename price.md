# Pricing Configuration

## Monetization Model: Paid Download

- **Price**: $1.99 (One-time purchase)
- **IAP**: Not required
- **Subscription**: None

## App Store Connect Pricing
- **Price Tier**: Tier 2 ($1.99)

## Pricing Justification
- Users save 50+ clicks/day × 365 days = 18,250 clicks/year
- $1.99 ÷ 18,250 = $0.0001 per click saved
- Competitor comparison: Super Agent $1.19/mo ($14.28/yr), 1Blocker $3.99/mo ($47.88/yr)
- $1.99 is the App Store "impulse buy" sweet spot
- Zero ongoing costs: pure local operation, rule updates via free GitHub Pages
- One-time purchase drives word-of-mouth ("Only 2 bucks, great value!")
