## Description
Cookie banners are the tax you pay for reading anything online. CookieZap removes that tax.

Instead of hiding banners and hoping the site behaves, CookieZap finds the consent dialog and chooses “Reject” or “Necessary only” for you — automatically, on every site in Safari.

**See it work in 10 seconds**
Open the built-in test page, watch the banner refuse itself, and come back to the app to see the count go up. No account. No setup beyond one Safari switch.

**Built on rules, not guesses**
CookieZap runs the open-source DuckDuckGo Autoconsent rule library: 1,609 consent platforms, including OneTrust, Cookiebot, Didomi, Usercentrics, Quantcast, Sourcepoint, TrustArc, Osano, CookieYes and Iubenda. That means it handles the multi-step “Manage choices” detours too, not just the easy banners.

**Know what happened on every page**
The Safari toolbar popup shows the current site’s status: protected, paused, banner detected, or “zapped”. You can also tap “Zap this page” to run it on demand.

**Three ways to protect**
• Smart Reject — finds the banner and taps Reject / Necessary only
• Hide Only — hides known banners without touching the page’s buttons
• Block All — hides banners and blocks known consent scripts at the network level

**Pause anywhere**
Add a site to the pause list in the app or straight from the Safari popup. Both stay in sync.

**Private by design**
Everything runs on your device. No account, no analytics, no browsing history leaves your iPhone or iPad. CookieZap only reads the page to find a cookie banner.

**One-time purchase**
No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Pay once, keep every future update.

Works in Safari on iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later). After installing, enable CookieZap in Settings › Safari › Extensions.

## Review Notes
CookieZap is a Safari web extension plus a small companion app.

How to test:
1. Open the app. On first launch, tap “Open Settings” and follow the steps: Settings › Safari › Extensions › CookieZap › turn on and allow.
2. In the app, tap “Test page” (https://asunnyboy861.github.io/CookieZap/test.html). The page shows a real cookie banner. With the extension enabled, the banner is refused or hidden automatically and the page reports success.
3. Open Safari’s toolbar popup on any site to see the per-site status and the “Zap this page” action.

Notes:
- The app is a one-time paid download. There are no in-app purchases.
- No account, login, or user data is required.
- CookieZap reads page content only to locate cookie consent dialogs. Nothing is transmitted off-device.
- The rule library is DuckDuckGo Autoconsent (MPL-2.0); attribution is available in Settings › Acknowledgements.
- If the extension does not appear in Safari settings, open the app once, then check Settings again.
